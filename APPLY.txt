WHY THE RESTRUCTURE

  - `pcbic/` was generic; the actual content is PCBIC v1.2 (April 30,
    1997). Promoting to `pcbic12/` makes the version explicit
    everywhere in paths.
  - `decrypted/` was a misleading name (implied we broke the
    encryption; really Clark's binaries were just packaged encrypted
    by a third party). `bin/` reflects what they ARE: Clark's original
    compiled binaries — reference targets for reconstruction.
  - `src/README.md` (new) documents the reconstruction source layout —
    what `pcbic.c` is now, and the per-binary subdir plan as Ghidra
    work lands.
